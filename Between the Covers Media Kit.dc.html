What Between the Covers Is

A revolt against the curated, the corporate, and the polished.

We tell the truth, even when it’s messy. Especially when it’s messy.

Real stories, raw voices

BTC champions the kind of reinvention you only earn by living through some shit — for women done performing perfection, and the allies beside them.

We don’t reach readers. We move them.

Every issue lands like a conversation women have been waiting to hear. Our stories make people feel seen — part of something bigger than their own chaos.

A Canadian movement, gone global

What started in Canada now sits in Barnes & Noble, in airport lounges, and in bookstores from Marbella to Dubai to Lahore. Honesty travels further than perfection.
