Before you book

Fair questions.

Who writes my feature?

Our editorial team. We interview you or read up on your business, then write it in the BTC voice. You check the facts before it goes live.

Will the feature link to my website?

Yes. Every partner feature on betweenthecoversmag.com links to your site. It's labelled as a partner feature and the link is tagged the way Google asks for paid placements, which keeps your site's search reputation safe.

What do I get for the $1,500 launch rate?

A full page in the BTC Global issue and an editorial feature on our site with a link to yours. Email, Instagram and app perks can be added from the rate card.

How will I know it worked?

We give you a tracked link for the feature and a QR code for print. Add a BTC offer code at your till or checkout and you can count every booking and sale that comes from us.

Will my ad sit next to a difficult story?

No. Ads never face stories about sexual violence, abuse or self-harm; those pages carry our own house ads. Tell us any other topic you want to stay clear of and we'll keep your page away from it.

Can I start with one placement?

Yes. Start with one page or one feature and grow from there. Four or six issues earn the repeat rates above.
