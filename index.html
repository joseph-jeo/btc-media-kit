Before you book

A few fair questions.

What is your click-through rate?

There isn’t one meaningful click-through rate for print, email, web and social combined. Ask for the recent results of the channel and placement you’re considering, including the date range and calculation. Web ad CTR generally compares ad clicks with ad impressions; email click rate often compares unique clickers with delivered emails. Social engagement is a separate metric.

Can you prove people visited my location or used my discount?

A dedicated code recorded at the till, a completed booking or a tracked check-in can establish a recorded response. Your team needs to capture and share those totals. QR scans, page views and directions clicks show interest; they do not independently prove a physical visit. Codes can also be shared, so report attributed responses without claiming every one was an additional sale caused by the ad.

Does search authority mean my ad will get results?

Search visibility can help people discover BTC’s editorial content. Third-party authority scores describe aspects of a website’s search profile; they aren’t Google endorsements or evidence of ad clicks, sales or visits. Campaign results need their own measurement.

Can I start with one placement?

Yes. The 2026 rate card includes one-time print rates and individual web or digital options. Choose a format, confirm the dates and agree a clear reader action before deciding whether to expand.

What happens after I enquire?

We discuss your business, audience, budget and timing, then put together a proposal with placements and pricing. The agreed booking confirms deliverables, deadlines, any creative services and measurement responsibilities. An enquiry does not reserve a placement or commit you to a purchase.
